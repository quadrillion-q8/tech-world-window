# TWW Search-Intent Upgrade — 2026-10-08

## Purpose
Strengthen topical relationships without creating thin pages or changing existing URLs.

## Changes
- Added automatic related-article discovery using shared tags, category, subcategory and pillar relationships.
- Preserved manually curated related articles as the first recommendations.
- Added contextual TWW Research connections for Windows, SSD/storage, RAM and gaming topics.
- Kept research claims separated from general guidance; no benchmark results are invented.

## Search-intent rules
- One primary intent per URL.
- Informational troubleshooting pages should answer the diagnostic question first.
- Commercial pages should focus on evaluation/selection rather than duplicating troubleshooting intent.
- Research pages should explain evidence/methodology and link back to practical guides.

## Next measurement step
Use Google Search Console query/page data after indexing to identify pages with impressions but weak CTR or average positions where title/H1/intro refinement is justified. Do not optimize from imagined keyword volume alone.
